The rain stopped just before sunrise. Mira opened the workshop door and
watched water run from the roof into a shallow stone basin. A sparrow landed
beside the basin, dipped its head, and vanished into the hedge.

On the workbench lay an old brass compass. Its needle had stuck against the
glass sometime during the winter. Mira loosened the screws and lifted the
cover. Underneath she found a grain of sand caught near the pivot. She drew
it away with a folded corner of paper. The needle swung north, then settled.

By noon the road was dry. Mira wrapped the compass in cloth and carried it
over the bridge. Her brother was waiting on the other side with a map and
two apples. They followed the river until the path began to climb.

At the top of the hill they could see the workshop roof and the bend in the
river. Mira set the compass on a flat rock. Her brother turned the map until
the river on the page lined up with the river below. A narrow path led east
through the trees. They ate their apples and walked on.
